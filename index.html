robo-Hermes

robo-Hermes 是一個只供本人使用的個人 AI 助理，在自己的電腦上執行。它透過 Google 授權讀取與新增本人 Google 日曆的行程，不對外提供服務，也不開放其他人使用。

robo-Hermes is a personal AI assistant used only by its owner and run on the owner's own computer. With the owner's Google authorization it reads and adds events in the owner's own Google Calendar. It is not offered to anyone else.
